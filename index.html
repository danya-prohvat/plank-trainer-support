❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a workout?

Open the app, select a program from the home screen, and tap the "Start" button to begin your training session.

Can I create my own custom program?

Yes! Go to the Programs tab and tap "Program Builder" to create your personalized workout routine.

How do I track my progress?

Your workout history is automatically saved. Visit the History tab to see all your completed sessions.

Can I change the exercise duration?

Yes! In the Program Builder, you can adjust the duration for each exercise from 1 second to 20 minutes.

The app isn't working properly

Try these steps:

  • Force close the app and reopen it
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store
  • Check if your iOS version is up to date
